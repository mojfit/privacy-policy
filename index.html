8. Account Deletion

MojFit provides in-app account deletion.

When deletion succeeds, MojFit attempts to remove:

  • Authentication account
  • User profile data
  • Fitness records
  • Meals, weight logs, photos, challenges and progress data

Backup systems may retain copies temporarily before automatic overwrite, typically within 30-90 days.
